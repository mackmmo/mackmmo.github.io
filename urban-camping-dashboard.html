Missoula Urban Camping Dashboard

Project Overview

This project was created by Mack Moore, Reya Worley, and Billy Li to help visualize the spatial impact of Missoula’s 2024 urban camping ordinances, adopted by the Missoula City Council. The project maps the buffer and location restrictions outlined in Missoula Municipal Code Section 12.60.030 to show where camping is prohibited and where it may still be allowed within city limits. Our goal was to make these regulations easier to understand by translating complex legal language into a clear, interactive spatial visualization.
