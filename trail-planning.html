Trail Planning: Mill Creek Climbing Area

Trail Planning Experience

Project Overview

The objective of this project was to explore trail construction options for the Mill Creek Climbing area. Mill Creek is a popular climbing area south of Missoula that has steep terrain and lengthy approaches to the 50+ climbing routes. The area is scheduled for trail construction this summer, pending grant funding, and presents a uniquely challenging construction site. Our analysis prioritizes trail paths which limit trail length, slope, elevation gain, and ultimately physical construction costs (length and construction difficulty), while promoting trail accessibility and ease of construction.
