Bio

I am a cartographer, geospatial analyst, and programmer living in Missoula, Montana. GIS is my main focus, especially where geospatial analysis and software development overlap. I feel an immense connection between data analysis and mapping to create applications that bring the power of GIS to anyone. As a graduate from the University of Montana with a GIS Certificate, I seek to add value and benefit to any project or organization with my own geospatial solutions.
